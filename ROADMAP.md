# Roadmap

What is done and what is next for AmoledSmartWatchOS (firmware) and the companion app. Each open
item has a short design so it can be picked up directly. Effort: S = an evening, M = a few days, L = a week or more.

## Next

### 1. Check fw 3.5 on the watch — S
Everything in 3.5 runs in the simulator and the emulator tests but not yet on hardware. Check: the double-tap window (0.3 s)
and stray taps on the face, Tiles paging, Bluetooth staying connected on the deep-idle parameters (iOS and Android), Health
Connect / Apple Health writes (the iPhone signing profile needs HealthKit), and a day's drain in Diagnostics.

### 2. Wake on motion from the IMU's interrupt — M
**Why:** the last screen-off polling left. Raise to wake still reads the accelerometer from the loop.
- Use the QMI8658's wake-on-motion engine (`configWakeOnMotion`) on INT1 and add it as a light-sleep GPIO wake source next to
  the touch controller and BOOT. The board file has `PIN_IMU_INT1 -1`: check the schematic (it may go through the IO expander,
  which can't wake the chip).
- Then the screen-off poll can go from 250 ms to about a second.

### 3. Gesture extras — S
- **Cover to sleep:** a palm over the screen (a large touch area, if the touch controller reports one) turns it off and silences an alarm.
- **Edge hints:** small marks at the face's edges for the first days, where the gestures are.
- **Hold BOOT:** choose the assistant or Emergency SOS (today: home).
- **Tiles on the watch:** reorder by touch and hold (today only from the app).

### 4. Memo processing with the app closed — M
The pipeline runs after each sync while the app's link service keeps it alive. A WorkManager job (Android) / BGProcessingTask
(iOS) could run it with the app swiped away; the on-phone models need to load in that job, within its time limit.

## Smaller ideas
- **Find my phone:** "last seen" location when the link drops (the phone sends its location on disconnect).
- **Phone controls:** camera shutter for the phone (link message → `MediaStore` intent with a countdown).
- **Weather tile:** the next hours under the current weather.
- **Battery:** a weekly report in the app (average drain by day, screen time against battery life).
